🛡️ Menveo Meningococcal Risk Dashboard

Meningococcal disease is often hidden by limited surveillance and underdiagnosis. This dashboard brings together the multiple factors that shape a child's exposure — region, season, accommodation, crowding, activities and individual factors — to make risk visible, discussable and actionable within the 2+ immunization conversation.

⚠️ This dashboard provides general educational risk estimates based on regional epidemiology (e.g., the African "meningitis belt"), seasonality, crowding/contact patterns and recognised individual risk factors. It is a discussion aid, not medical advice or a diagnostic tool. Clinical decisions and vaccination recommendations should follow national immunization guidelines and individual patient assessment. Consult current WHO/CDC and local health authority guidance for outbreak data.
