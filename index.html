🌍 Meningococcal Travel Risk Assessor

Select or type any travel destination to assess meningococcal disease exposure — from direct regional prevalence and from travelers arriving from higher-risk countries.

⚠️ This tool provides general educational estimates based on regional epidemiology (e.g., the African "meningitis belt"), pilgrimage/mass-gathering patterns, and traveler inflow trends. It is not medical advice. Consult a travel medicine clinic or the WHO/CDC before travel for current outbreak data and vaccine recommendations.
